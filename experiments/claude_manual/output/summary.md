# Structural studies on Multiferroic La1-xBixCrO3 Perovskites

**Authors:** Aga Shahee, Dhirendra Kumar, N. P. Lalla  
**DOI:** 10.1063/1.3606316  
**Model:** claude-manual

The authors made five La1-xBixCrO3 ceramics (x = 0 to 0.35) by solid-state reaction and studied their crystal structure with XRD and TEM. Rietveld refinement shows every sample is a single-phase orthorhombic perovskite (space group Pnma) with Bi on the La site. The lattice parameters and cell volume change non-monotonically with Bi content, which the authors attribute to the Bi3+ lone pair distorting Bi-O bonds and tilting the CrO6 octahedra. TEM/SAED on x = 0.3 confirms the Pnma cell.

## Rietveld refinement

- **[E002] S1, S2, S3, S4, S5** (page 2): FullProf-2K Rietveld refinement starting from the reported LaCrO3 model with Bi placed on the La site; all peaks indexed by a single orthorhombic Pnma phase. Lattice parameters change non-monotonically with x.
  - space group: Pnma
  - crystal system: orthorhombic
  - Goldschmidt tolerance factor range: 0.84092 to 0.84072
- **[E003] S1, S2, S3, S4, S5** (page 2): Trends vs Bi content: a increases up to x = 0.30 then drops at 0.35; b and cell volume decrease to x = 0.20, increase to 0.30, then drop abruptly; c decreases slowly to 0.30 and faster above. Attributed to anisotropic Bi-O bonds from the Bi3+ lone pair causing octahedral tilting (La3+ 1.32 Å and Bi3+ 1.30 Å radii are nearly equal).
  - ionic radius La3+: 1.32 Å
  - ionic radius Bi3+: 1.30 Å
- **[E004] S4** (page 2): A typical Rietveld fit is shown for x = 0.3.
- **[E008] S4** (Fig. 1b panel b (page 1)): Rietveld fit: Observed vs calculated pattern, Bragg positions and difference curve for x = 0.30. Difference curve is nearly flat, indicating a good single-phase Pnma fit.
